# MODEL.md — "The One-Day Gazette": Wednesday 18 November 2026

## What this entry is

A dense newspaper front page ("The One-Day Gazette") summing up the world
news of **Wednesday 18 November 2026** in one 1024x1024 image: a masthead
with the date, a lead story with a flat drawn illustration, 40 further
stories grouped by region and field, a UTC timeline of the day's key
moments, and a date-check footer. 41 stories in total.

## Why this version (v2)

The v1 entry for this competition was a simple poster with a handful of
stories. It ranks far outside the top-50 paid zone. The entries leading
this competition's board are dense front pages with 40-50 sourced stories,
several regions, a UTC timeline and an explicit date check. This v2 follows
that verified pattern: same day, same competition, but a genuinely denser,
better-sourced front page built from reporting dated 18 November 2026.

## Lead story — why it leads

The day's most consequential story is the Gulf exchange: the United States
struck an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island, and Iran answered in the
early hours of Wednesday with drones aimed at American installations in
Kuwait and Bahrain. It combines an active military exchange, Gulf shipping
and oil risk, and the run-up to the Xi-Putin-Trump meeting the next day.
Sources: Reuters, Wall Street Journal, Al Jazeera, Just Security's
18 November Early Edition.

## What was excluded and why

- **Quad leaders' summit in Manila.** Widely mentioned in commentary,
  but the leaders' meeting itself took place on **17 November**
  (White House / ASEAN summit monitor). Only the 18 November follow-on
  — Trump's trade-deal claim at the Marcos luncheon — is included.
- **US strike on Qeshm Island as a Wednesday event.** The strike itself
  was on Tuesday; it is in the lead only as the stated cause of Iran's
  Wednesday drone launches, which is how 18 November reporting frames it.
- **Pulisic's bicycle kick / USMNT Nations League win.** The match was
  played on Tuesday 17 November; the reporting is dated the 17th, so it
  stays out even though it was still being discussed on the 18th.
- **Houthi drone claim (17 November).** Included only as a claim first
  reported on 18 November, worded "Houthis say they fired drones".
- **Sudan power-grid strikes.** The attacks were on Monday; the Al
  Jazeera report is dated 18 November, so the event is noted in the
  timeline as "reported" rather than as a Wednesday attack.
- **Anthropic IPO pricing.** Reported on 18 November as the day's
  listing; the record $81.3bn raise itself was reported on 19 November
  and is not claimed here.
- **Ecuador rains (91,222 people affected), Assam bus crash (24-26
  dead), Gaza flooding figures.** Verified 18 November stories cut for
  space; the grid holds 40 stories and these were the closest cuts.

## How the image was made

`draw_frontpage.py` (PIL only) renders the page: masthead, lead story
with a flat drawn illustration built from basic shapes (land, strait,
ship, drones, burst — no copied artwork or photos), a three-column grid
with one-line summaries, a UTC timeline strip and a date-check footer.
Text is wrapped by measured pixel width, so nothing overflows or
garbles. `main.py` (Python standard library only) copies the rendered
`entry.png` to the output directory and verifies the copy byte for byte.
